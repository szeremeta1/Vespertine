**Volume keys and the AirPods Max crown control what you're hearing.**

### Fixed
- **The Digital Crown and volume keys now adjust the device Nocturne plays to.**
  - Before this release, Nocturne took sole (exclusive) control of the device. macOS then moved the Mac's sound output to a different device, so the crown on AirPods Max and the volume keys changed that other device instead, such as a DAC selected in Control Center.
  - Nocturne now shares the device by default and makes it the Mac's sound output while playing. The crown, the volume keys and Control Center all act on what you're hearing, and the volume slider stays in sync.
- **Volume no longer jumps after you pause and press play.** The crown's changes were never recorded by the Mac, so when Nocturne took the device back after a pause, the Mac put back its stale level.

### Changed
- **Bit-perfect without exclusive access.** Nocturne still sets the device's format for every track, and playback is bit-perfect whenever nothing else is playing through the same device. If another app plays through it at the same time, macOS mixes the two, and the signal path says **MIXED WITH OTHER APPS** instead of claiming bit-perfect.
- **Exclusive access is still available** (Settings → Output, or the output menu) if you'd rather silence other apps on the device. Settings explains that macOS then won't let the crown or volume keys reach it. DSD over DoP always takes exclusive access.

### Install
Download **Nocturne-0.5.2.dmg**, or let Nocturne update itself. Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.
