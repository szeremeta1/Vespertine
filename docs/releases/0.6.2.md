**Volume keys that reach your DAC, albums that list each song once, and skipping that never stalls playback.**

### Improved
- **Volume keys, Control Center and the AirPods Max crown reach the device Vespertine plays on, even with exclusive access.** macOS never makes a device another app holds exclusively the Mac's sound output, so the keys used to change whatever device macOS picked instead. Playing on AirPods Max, for example, they changed the speakers. While Vespertine holds a device, volume and mute now go to that device, and the sound output takes its level, so each key press steps from where the volume really is. When Vespertine lets the device go, the sound output gets its own volume and mute back.
- **The Mac's own speakers and headphone jack always play shared.** Exclusive access gains nothing there: they only take float formats, and shared mode still plays bit-perfect (and says so) while no other app is using them. It also kept the volume keys from reaching them. External DACs are unchanged.
- **Albums list each song once, in order.** A song can be in your library twice: in *Imported Music* and on a network share, or as two copies of the same edition. It now shows as one row, marked "+ COPY" or with the other copy's format ("+ 16/44.1", "+ MP3"). Playback picks the best copy: lossless first, then channels, sample rate and bit depth, and a local copy before a network one.
  - Songs are listed in disc and track order. A song with no disc number counts as disc 1 instead of jumping to the top.
  - Vinyl-style positions ("A1") no longer show every track as 0. Missing track numbers are read from file names that can't be misread ("05 Title", "… - 05 - Title"), and a missing disc number from a numbered disc folder ("CD 02").
- **Multi-disc album pages number tracks disc·track** ("1·10", "2·1"…), so you can see where the next disc begins.

### Fixed
- **Skipping to a song at another sample rate could stop playback for good,** with "Waiting for … to reconnect" while the DAC was still connected. Reconfiguring the DAC several times in quick succession could leave it paused for Vespertine. Vespertine now keeps hold of the DAC between songs and lets each change finish before the next one. If an output ever does refuse to start, Vespertine says so plainly instead of waiting forever.
- **Some songs lost their fake hi-res verdict** and never got it back, because their stored analysis counted as done. Verdicts are now restored from the stored analysis.
- **Songs in network-share folders with characters SMB can't carry (`?`, `:`) got no server analysis.** They are now matched by size and date.

### Install
Download **Vespertine-0.6.2.dmg**, or let Vespertine update itself. Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.
