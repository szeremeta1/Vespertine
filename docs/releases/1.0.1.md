**Vespertine 1.0.1.** The free, open-source Mac player for surround and hi-res music collections.

If you're new here: Vespertine plays your stereo and surround music from one library (FLAC, DSD, SACD and DVD-Video images, DTS CDs, Dolby TrueHD, DTS-HD Master Audio, Atmos from Dolby Digital Plus), shows every step between the file and your DAC, measures and corrects your room with a USB microphone, and tells you whether your "hi-res" files really are. No account, no telemetry, no subscription.

### New since 0.15.1

- **Speaker Groups (beta): several USB DACs as one speaker system.** Play one stream across two or more DACs as a quad or surround setup, with a channel map, a drawing of your room, and level, delay and polarity for each speaker. One DAC keeps the clock and the others follow it. Off by default (Settings > Playback > Speaker Groups (beta)); with it off nothing changes. It is a beta: tested with automated tests and virtual devices, and by hand on one real pair of DACs (Fosi Audio ZD3 and FiiO K11). Only the clock leader's channels can be bit-perfect, and the signal path says which. [Details and limits](https://github.com/szeremeta1/Vespertine/blob/main/docs/SPEAKER-GROUPS.md)
- **Fill the room.** A switch for each group. A stereo song stays on the front pair as it is, and the back pair plays the song's own ambience a few milliseconds later, so the image stays in front. No reverb is added. The signal path shows ROOM RENDER while it's on.
- **Spatialize stereo.** A switch for each output, off by default. Stereo and mono songs are upmixed to 5.1: through Spatial Audio on AirPods and Beats, or across every speaker on a multichannel output.
- **Multichannel plays channel for channel.** Unlabeled 5.1, 7.1, 12- and 16-channel files now reach the right speakers, and a file with more speakers than your setup is folded into the nearest ones at the standard -3 dB instead of losing channels.
- **Eleven VU and PPM meter designs in the Visualizer,** modelled on real instruments: broadcast, studio console, reel-to-reel and 1970s cassette deck VU meters, and BBC, EBU, DIN and Nordic PPMs. The Visualizer is off until you switch it on, and it never touches the sound.
- **Room correction keeps profiles.** Several per output, with Guided and Advanced views, and a more reliable timing reference in small rooms.
- **Network shares.** Songs start sooner (a DSD song no longer waits for the whole file), the copy shows its progress, and skip, stop and seek keep working when a share stops answering. A streamed FLAC now ends at the end of the file and the next song follows, without keeping a processor core busy.
- **Volume Up, Volume Down and Mute** in the Controls menu (⌘↑, ⌘↓, ⌥⌘↓).
- **Remove Missing Songs…** in the File menu. It asks first and never deletes a file.

### Changed

- **Scanning and analyzing new music are now opt-in.** "Scan for new music automatically" and "Analyze new music automatically" are off after you update, even if you had them on. Turn them on in Settings, or press Rescan Library or Analyze Library whenever you want.

### Fixed

A long round of fixes to playback, the signal path, devices, decoding, the library, accessibility and translations. The ones you're most likely to notice: a pause right after play stays silent, quick play and pause presses while the next song loads no longer confuse the player, a file folded onto a different speaker layout is no longer called BIT-PERFECT, and a library scan no longer leaves a new album with only its first few tracks. Skipping quickly through songs on a slow network share no longer leaves you waiting on songs you skipped past; a share that stops answering no longer freezes the app at a skip or a song change; sorting a very large library no longer freezes the app; and waiting for the next song's first bytes is shown as buffering instead of counted as thousands of buffer underruns.

### Not tested yet

- Multichannel USB DACs and AV receivers (one Sony TV over HDMI has been tried). Dolby and DTS bitstream to a receiver is experimental.
- Intel Macs on real hardware (the Intel build has run under Rosetta).
- DSD512 over DoP on hardware. DoP works up to DSD256 on a Fosi Audio ZD3 and DSD128 on a FiiO K11.
- Speaker Groups from the app on hardware by measurement: a bit-exact leader, follower quality, a real unplug, an hour of playback.
- The new translations have not been reviewed by native speakers. Corrections are welcome.

If you have any of that hardware, a [device report](https://github.com/szeremeta1/Vespertine/issues/new?template=dac_report.yml) helps more than anything else.

### Install

Download **Vespertine-1.0.1.dmg**, or `brew install --cask szeremeta1/tap/vespertine`. macOS 14.4 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.

Already installed? Vespertine updates itself, or run `brew upgrade --cask vespertine`.
