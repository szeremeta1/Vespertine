**Vespertine 1.1.4.** The free, open-source Mac player for surround and hi-res music collections.

If you're new here: Vespertine plays your stereo and surround music from one library (FLAC, DSD, SACD and DVD-Video images, DTS CDs, Dolby TrueHD, DTS-HD Master Audio, Atmos from Dolby Digital Plus), shows every step between the file and your DAC, measures and corrects your room with a USB microphone, and tells you whether your "hi-res" files really are. No account, no telemetry, no subscription.

### Fixed

- AirPods and other Bluetooth headphones: when another app opens their microphone, macOS drops them into call mode (low sample rate, mono, no Spatial Audio). Vespertine now says so on the output pill and in the Inspector, and names the app holding the microphone.
- Switching AirPods between Bluetooth and USB-C, or into call mode, no longer drops about a second of Atmos audio, no longer shows "Lossless" while the headphones are really in call mode, and no longer leaves a stale warning behind.
- A song playing from a network share no longer skips to the next one when a single read fails. Vespertine retries, and if the share really is gone it says so instead of reporting a decoding error.
- When a share stops answering mid-song, the Inspector and a banner now say so after 10 seconds while Vespertine keeps trying.

### Visualizer

- Beat strobe actually flashes on the beat.
- Club and Particles move with the music, driven by a new beat detector. Club Classic is redrawn.
- Album Art picks the vivid colours from the cover, and black-and-white covers draw in their own greys.
- The oscilloscope holds steady, and the spectrogram's edge flicker and flat colours are fixed.

### Install

Download **Vespertine-1.1.4.dmg**, or `brew install --cask szeremeta1/tap/vespertine`. macOS 14.4 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.

Already installed? Vespertine updates itself, or run `brew upgrade --cask vespertine`.
