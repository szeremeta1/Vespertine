**Vespertine 1.0.0 is the stable release of the free, open-source Mac player for surround and hi-res music: surround files and disc images in one library, a signal path you can check, and room correction measured in your own room.**

### What 1.0 is
- **The same code as 0.15.1.** Everything since 0.14.2 is in it: Transitions, every copy of an album, the Atmos and DTS meters and the Album Art colours from 0.15.0, and the new formats and fixes from 0.15.1. 1.0 marks the features below as the ones Vespertine ships with, tested on the hardware listed under Limits. Fixes after this one come as 1.0.x releases.
- **Free and open source.** GPL-3.0, no account, no telemetry. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.

### What it does
- **Stereo and surround in one library.** Extracted SACD 5.1, multichannel FLAC, DTS CDs, Dolby TrueHD and DTS-HD Master Audio sit next to your stereo albums. Every edition of an album shows on one card.
- **Every copy of an album.** When your library has an album more than once (a hi-res download and a vinyl transfer, stereo and 5.1 or Atmos mixes, an SACD beside the CD), its page lists every copy with its format, layout, edition and place. Any copy of a song can be played on any output, favorited, or starred as the one to prefer. Copies are never merged unless you bundle them; bundled, each song plays the copy your output supports.
- **Disc images, played directly.** SACD `.iso` images (stereo and multichannel, DST or plain) and DVD-Video LPCM (`.iso` or a `VIDEO_TS` folder) play straight from the image, with nothing mounted or extracted. DST-compressed DSDIFF files and EAC CUE sheets are supported.
- **A signal path you can check.** Decoding, resampling, mixing, gain and output format are shown for every track, read back from Core Audio. BIT-PERFECT appears only when the conditions in the code are met, and when something changed the samples the path says what. A built-in loopback test checks the software path sample by sample.
- **Room correction.** Measure your speakers with a UMIK-1 or another calibrated USB microphone at one to five spots, and Vespertine fits filters to each speaker and lines up their delay and level. The microphone records at a rate it offers, and the output's rate is never changed for the measurement. Stereo and 5.1 PCM. It imports and exports Equalizer APO and REW filters and plays impulse responses. Off by default; with it off, playback is untouched.
- **Native DSD.** DSF and DSDIFF from DSD64 to DSD512, and DSD in WavPack, go out as DoP to DACs that take it, or convert to high-rate PCM, and the signal path says which.
- **More formats.** FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, APE, WavPack, TTA and TAK, the common lossy formats, and the audio in Matroska and WebM files (one track per audio stream). Cue sheets inside FLAC files become separate tracks. Formats it can't decode, such as Dolby AC-4 and MPEG-H 3D Audio, are named as such instead of failing with a generic error.
- **Transitions, off until you turn them on.** Crossfade, Smart Mix (it measures the ends of your songs and picks a blend for each pair) and Live Set (for hosting, with beat-matched blends between songs with a steady tempo). Albums, CUE sheets, SACDs and the genres you exclude always play gapless, and by default a transition never changes your output format. Off, every song plays exactly as before, bit for bit.
- **Spatial Audio for your own files.** Local surround, 5.1 DSD included, plays as head-tracked Spatial Audio on AirPods, with Atmos objects rendered from Dolby Digital Plus. Surround can also be exported as binaural stereo or multichannel ALAC.
- **Fake hi-res detection.** Flags 16-bit audio padded to 24, likely upsampled masters and likely lossy sources, with the measurements behind each verdict.
- **Channel meters for Atmos and bitstreams.** Dolby Atmos rendered by macOS, and Dolby Digital, Dolby Digital Plus and DTS sent untouched to a receiver, show their channel bed and spectrum, from a second decode that never touches what goes to the output.
- **The Visualizer.** Eight styles, starting with a spinning record, in a window of its own, and eight colour themes. The newest, **Album Art**, draws in up to five colours taken from the cover of the song playing and fades to the next cover's colours when the song changes. A grayscale, blank or missing cover falls back to the default colours. It reads a copy of the samples, so the output to your DAC is bit-for-bit identical with it on and off. Off by default.
- **Network playback.** Tracks on a share are copied to your Mac before they play, and the next track is copied ahead.
- **Equalizer, Up Next, History and more.** The equalizer says when it can't change what's playing, with an optional reference equalizer for each USB-C AirPods Max model. Optional Discord status.
- **44 languages besides English,** including right-to-left layouts for Arabic, Hebrew, Persian and Urdu. The translations are machine-quality and haven't been checked by native speakers.

### Limits
- **Channel-for-channel output** is confirmed on one real device, a Sony TV over HDMI (5.1 and a 7.1 to 5.1 downmix). Multichannel USB DACs and Dolby or DTS bitstream to receivers are untested on real hardware.
- **Intel Macs are untested** on hardware. The Intel build has run under Rosetta.
- **DSD over DoP** is confirmed up to DSD256 on the Fosi Audio ZD3 and up to DSD128 on the FiiO K11. DSD512 over DoP is untested on hardware.
- **Room correction** covers stereo and 5.1 PCM that Vespertine renders. Dolby and DTS bitstreams, Atmos and other channel counts step aside, and the signal path says why. Measuring with a microphone at its own rate was tested with simulated devices and a loopback output, not yet on a real UMIK-1 with an external DAC.
- **Transitions:** nobody has listened to a beat-matched Live Set blend yet, and the bass swap, Live Set's fixed rate and the DSD gap have not been run through a real DAC. Beat detection finds a steady tempo at about four song ends in ten.
- **The Atmos and DTS meters** show the bed, not the Atmos objects, may run a little ahead of what a receiver plays, and have not been run against a real receiver.
- **Not supported:** audio effect plugins, streaming services, UPnP/DLNA, DVD-Audio and DRM-protected Apple Music downloads. Dolby AC-4 and MPEG-H 3D Audio files are named but not played. MQA files play as the plain FLAC they contain, not unfolded. TrueHD Atmos and DTS:X objects are not rendered.
- Reports from your hardware are what move these lines. The device report form is in Issues.

### Verification
- Test run on the 1.0.0 release candidate: the app tests passed 57 of 57. A few timing-only tests failed in the full-suite run and passed when re-run on their own.
- The rest of the verification record is in `docs/VERIFICATION.md`, the tested devices are in `docs/DEVICES.md`, and each claim and its evidence is in `verification/CLAIMS.md`.

### Install
Download **Vespertine-1.0.0.dmg**, or let Vespertine update itself. Requires macOS 14.4 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled. Homebrew: `brew upgrade --cask vespertine`.
