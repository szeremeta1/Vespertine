**Vespertine 0.13.2 plays 5.1 DSD in Spatial Audio on AirPods, tells you when audio is converted or downmixed, and no longer hangs on quit.**

### Spatial Audio
- **5.1 DSD now plays in Spatial Audio.** A 5.1 DSD file (DSF, DSDIFF, DST-compressed DSDIFF or an SACD image) is converted to PCM and every channel is placed where it belongs, so the LFE and surround channels are no longer lost. Before, discrete DSDIFF channels could land on a three-speaker bed and the rear channels went missing.
- **It says what happened to the audio.** The signal path shows **DSD → PCM** next to SPATIAL or a downmix, and two new notices, "Converted from DSD" and "Downmixed to stereo", appear when they apply. Both are translated into all 44 languages.
- Checked by the developer on AirPods Max.

### Fixed
- **Quitting no longer hangs.** Cmd-Q could leave a spinning cursor while Vespertine waited on the audio device. It now quits right away.
- **Multichannel files read correctly.** RF64 and Wave64 files open properly, DSD channel names are read from the file, and discrete multichannel routing follows the file's own channel layout.

### Verification
- The engine, library and app test suites pass, including new tests that play each 5.1 DSD format through Spatial Audio and check that every channel arrives in its place. The left/right placement check runs on real Macs only; GitHub's test machines skip that one check.

### Install
Download **Vespertine-0.13.2.dmg**, or let Vespertine update itself. Requires macOS 14.4 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled. Homebrew: `brew upgrade --cask vespertine`.
