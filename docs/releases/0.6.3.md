**A smooth AirPods Max crown, and skipping that lands on the song you pressed.**

### Fixed
- **Turning the AirPods Max crown made the volume jitter back and forth while Vespertine held them with exclusive access.** Since 0.6.2 the volume moves between the AirPods and the Mac's sound output in both directions. A change could come back from the other side still showing the old level, and Vespertine copied that back, undoing most of the crown's steps. Vespertine now recognizes these echoes of its own changes, so the crown, the volume keys and Control Center each move the volume once.
- **After taking the AirPods Max off and putting them back on, the volume keys stopped reaching them.** macOS removes them while they are off your head, and Vespertine didn't reconnect the volume keys when they came back. It now does.
- **Pressing Next several times quickly, on a network share, could land on a song several skips back.** Vespertine opened every song skipped past in turn, and each one it reached moved the queue back to itself. Now only the song you end on is opened, the queue and Now Playing agree on it, and the share stops copying songs you skipped past.

### Install
Download **Vespertine-0.6.3.dmg**, or let Vespertine update itself. Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.
