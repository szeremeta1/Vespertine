**Vespertine 0.14.2 makes the Visualizer's meter lights behave like the real thing, draws the spectrum for Dolby Atmos rendered by macOS, and shows newly plugged-in output devices straight away.**

### Visualizer
- **The meters' red lights work.** In the Meters style, the red light at the top right of each meter now comes on in two cases: when the needle is in the red, or when the music truly clips (a sample peak of -1 dBFS or more). It stays lit for about a quarter of a second afterwards. Before, it could stay dark however loud the music got.
- **The needle decides, so the sensitivity control counts.** On the VU meter the red starts at 0 VU, the band printed in red on the dial. The PPM prints no red, so its light starts at mark 6, the highest peak the BBC allows. Turning the sensitivity control up moves the needles, and the lights with them. Turning it down never hides a real clip.
- **Atmos now draws the spectrum.** When macOS renders Dolby Atmos, the Visualizer used to show nothing. While the Visualizer window is open, Vespertine now decodes a second copy of the track in the background, a stereo mix of the Dolby bed, and draws that. With the window closed nothing extra is decoded, and the audio macOS gets is untouched. The picture shows the bed, not the height of the objects or Spatial Audio processing, and it may run a few tens of milliseconds ahead of what you hear.

### Output devices
- **New devices show up when you plug them in.** If you plugged in a USB device while Vespertine was running, it could be missing from the output list until you chose it as the Mac's sound output in the menu bar. Vespertine now notices when a new device finishes setting up its outputs and adds it to the list. It never changes the Mac's sound output.

### Verification
- The engine, analysis and app test suites pass. New tests render the meters offscreen and read the light's pixel in every case above, check the Atmos bed decoding on a Dolby Digital Plus 5.1 test file, and check device hot-plug against a simulated Core Audio.
- Not yet checked: Atmos on a real Atmos file (a JOC stream) and how well the bars line up with a device's delay; a real USB device, such as AirPods Max over USB-C, plugged in while Vespertine runs (a device that takes more than 15 seconds to finish setting up its outputs and sends no notice could still be missed); and the meter lights on real music at the default sensitivity.

### Install
Download **Vespertine-0.14.2.dmg**, or let Vespertine update itself. Requires macOS 14.4 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled. Homebrew: `brew upgrade --cask vespertine`.
