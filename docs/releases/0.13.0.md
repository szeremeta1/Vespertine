**Vespertine now keeps a history of what you've played, gives Up Next its own panel beside the library, shows every edition of an album on one card, and can show what you're playing as your Discord status.**

### Up Next and History
- **Up Next is a panel now.** It opens on the right of the window, where the Inspector goes, instead of a popover that closed on any click. It lists Now Playing, then **Playing Next** (songs you added with Play Next or Add to Queue, in the order you added them), then **Continue Playing** (the rest of what you started). Add to Queue now lands before the album carries on, not after it. Drag rows to reorder, remove them with the button on hover or the Delete key, and play one with Return or a double-click. Shuffle mixes only the rest; Playing Next stays put.
- **History.** Every song you listen to for 10 seconds or more (or half of it, if it's shorter) is listed, most recent first, grouped by day. Play it again, queue it, or remove it; clearing History leaves play counts alone. Upgrading doesn't start you from an empty list: each song's last play is carried in.
- View menu: Show/Hide Up Next (⌥⌘U) and Show/Hide History.

### Discord
- **Discord status (optional, off by default).** Turn on **Show what's playing as my Discord status** in Settings › Online, and while a song plays your Discord status shows its title, artist, album, format (such as 24/96 FLAC or DSD128) and progress, through the Discord app on your Mac. It clears when you pause or quit. Covers are looked up by album name on the Cover Art Archive or in Apple's music catalog, because Discord can only show images from the web; your own files are never uploaded. You can turn covers off separately.

### Albums
- **Editions on one card.** Albums titled with mastering, pressing or reissue labels, such as *(DCC Mastering)*, *(2009 Re-Mastered)*, *(Japan SHM-CD)*, *(Expanded)* or *(… Anniversary)*, now join their album's editions card instead of showing as a separate album.

### Fixed
- **Play counts.** While a newly chosen or gapless next song was opening, the previous song's listening time could count a play, scrobble or history entry for the new one. Each now counts only for the song that was actually heard.
- **DoP and bitstream output.** A frame too large for its IEC 61937 burst, a damaged frame in an MP4 file, or a short final Dolby Digital Plus group now plays as silence of the same length instead of being sent malformed to the receiver. An odd last byte of a DSD file is padded with DSD silence instead of being dropped.

### Install
Download **Vespertine-0.13.0.dmg**, or let Vespertine update itself. Requires macOS 14.4 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled. Homebrew: `brew upgrade --cask vespertine`.
