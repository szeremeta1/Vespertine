**Vespertine now works properly in Light Mode, lets outputs without a volume control use Vespertine's volume, and keeps analyzing your library while you listen from a network share.**

### Volume
- **Volume for fixed-volume outputs.** HDMI, optical and line-out DACs have no volume of their own, so Vespertine used to leave them at full level. Click the FIXED badge in the bottom bar, or open the output's device panel, and turn on **Volume control in Vespertine** to set the level in the app. Each output remembers its own choice and level, and the switch in Settings stays the default for every other output. Below 100% the output is no longer bit-perfect, and the labels say so.

### File analysis
- **Analysis keeps going while a share plays.** With a song playing from a network share, analyzing a selection or the whole library stayed at 0 until the music stopped. Analysis now reads one share file at a time alongside the song, steps aside while a song starts or its buffer runs low, and runs at full speed once the playing song is fully copied to the local cache.

### Fixed
- **Light Mode.** With macOS set to Light Mode, the menu bar window showed dark text on its dark panel, and some popovers, alerts and menus didn't match the rest of the app. Vespertine now draws dark everywhere, whatever the system appearance. Thanks for the report in [#68](https://github.com/szeremeta1/Vespertine/issues/68).

### Also new
- A small “Made with ❤️ in Boston” line on both About screens.

### Known issues
- A stray icon can appear next to the sidebar button while the search field is active.

### Install
Download **Vespertine-0.12.2.dmg**, or let Vespertine update itself. Requires macOS 14.4 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled. Homebrew: `brew upgrade --cask vespertine`.
