**Vespertine 1.1.1.** The free, open-source Mac player for surround and hi-res music collections.

If you're new here: Vespertine plays your stereo and surround music from one library (FLAC, DSD, SACD and DVD-Video images, DTS CDs, Dolby TrueHD, DTS-HD Master Audio, Atmos from Dolby Digital Plus), shows every step between the file and your DAC, measures and corrects your room with a USB microphone, and tells you whether your "hi-res" files really are. No account, no telemetry, no subscription.

### New in 1.1.1

- **A new web remote.** The page you get when you open the remote in a browser on your network is redesigned, with a darker look in black and brass, and it now does much more than control playback:
  - **Library.** Browse your albums and songs from the browser.
  - **Playlists.** Open a playlist and play it.
  - **Queue.** See what's coming up and drag songs into a new order.
  - **Search.** Search songs, artists and albums, with the results grouped.
  - **Home-screen app.** Add it to the home screen of a phone or tablet and it opens like an app, without the browser bars.
  - It follows the Mac's language, in the same 44 languages as the app.

### Fixed

- The volume slider is smooth again with Mac Sound Output on. Every step of the slider was asking macOS about the Sound Output helper, which made it stutter.

### Known issues

- Mac Sound Output (beta): when stereo from other apps is upmixed to surround, the back channels play very quietly.
- Mac Sound Output (beta): with the output fixed at 5.1, the rear-surround channels of a 7.1 file are dropped instead of folded in.
- While Mac Sound Output is playing, the Now Playing panel shows "Speakers: Not set up".
- If the Mac Sound Output helper is quit by force, it has not yet been checked that it restarts by itself.

### Not tested yet

- The web remote from a real phone: the new page has been tested in Chromium and WebKit against a pretend Mac, not yet on an iPhone or in Safari on one. The home-screen app is the part most worth checking.
- Mac Sound Output on real DACs from the app.
- Multichannel USB DACs and AV receivers (one Sony TV over HDMI has been tried). Dolby and DTS bitstream to a receiver is experimental.

If you have any of that hardware, a [device report](https://github.com/szeremeta1/Vespertine/issues/new?template=dac_report.yml) helps more than anything else.

### Install

Download **Vespertine-1.1.1.dmg**, or `brew install --cask szeremeta1/tap/vespertine`. macOS 14.4 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.

Already installed? Vespertine updates itself, or run `brew upgrade --cask vespertine`.
