**Vespertine 1.1.3.** The free, open-source Mac player for surround and hi-res music collections.

If you're new here: Vespertine plays your stereo and surround music from one library (FLAC, DSD, SACD and DVD-Video images, DTS CDs, Dolby TrueHD, DTS-HD Master Audio, Atmos from Dolby Digital Plus), shows every step between the file and your DAC, measures and corrects your room with a USB microphone, and tells you whether your "hi-res" files really are. No account, no telemetry, no subscription.

### New

- The iPhone and iPad remote now has Sources, Genres, Recently Added and Songs, like the Mac app. Update the Mac to 1.1.3 so these pages fill in.
- The remote's volume slider follows your finger live instead of jumping when you let go.
- Favorites with several copies of one song (for example ALAC and Atmos) show once, with the copies a tap away, the way the Mac app does.

### Install

Download **Vespertine-1.1.3.dmg**, or `brew install --cask szeremeta1/tap/vespertine`. macOS 14.4 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.

Already installed? Vespertine updates itself, or run `brew upgrade --cask vespertine`.
