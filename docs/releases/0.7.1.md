**After DSD, your DAC goes back to its own sample rate.**

### Fixed
- **A DAC no longer stays at the DoP carrier rate after DSD.** When Vespertine lets go of a DAC it was playing DSD to (after a pause longer than "Release device after pausing for", a stop, or the end of the queue), the DAC goes back to the sample rate it had before Vespertine changed it. Before, it stayed at 176.4 or 352.8 kHz and showed that rate, and whatever played through it next was resampled to it. Skips and seeks between songs are unchanged and add no rate switch.

### Install
Download **Vespertine-0.7.1.dmg**, or let Vespertine update itself. Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.
