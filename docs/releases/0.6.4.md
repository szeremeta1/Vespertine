**Filters on every page, and Shuffle for genres, artists and everything else.**

### New
- **Every page can be filtered.** Albums, Artists, Songs, Genres, Recently Added, Favorites, playlists, sources, each artist's and genre's page, and search share one filter bar. The format chips (FLAC, WAV / AIFF, ALAC, DSD, Dolby & DTS, lossy, 24-bit, 88.2 kHz and up, multichannel) now combine, so "FLAC or ALAC, 24-bit, 88.2 kHz and up" is three clicks, and a Favorites chip joins them.
- **A Filters panel for the rest:** genre, year, artist, format, exact sample rate (down to DSD64 or DSD128), bit depth, channels (stereo, 5.1, 7.1…), analysis verdict and source. Each choice shows how many albums or songs it would leave, and genres and artists can be searched. What you pick shows as a tag under the chips, one click to remove.
- **Artists and Genres follow the filter:** they list only those with matching albums, counted by those albums. A genre or artist opened from a filtered page keeps the filter, and each page keeps its own between launches.
- **Shuffle and Play everywhere:** on every page, and in the menus of artists, genres, albums, playlists and the sidebar's library items. They play what the page shows, filter included. With Multichannel on, the 5.1 versions play even on a stereo DAC.

### Changed
- **Shuffle starts at a random song** instead of always the first. **Play plays in order** and turns shuffle off, as in Music; double-clicking a song keeps your shuffle setting.
- The Albums page's Genre and Decade menus are now in the Filters panel.
- Playing or shuffling a whole library takes under a second.
- Find Music shows how much a copy will really take and how much space is free, and won't start one that would leave less than 2 GB.
- MusicBrainz lookups now tell MusicBrainz which version of Vespertine is asking and where to reach its author, as MusicBrainz asks.

### Fixed
- Strict code-signature verification (`codesign --verify --deep --strict`) failed on the app installed from the disk image, because of a Finder flag the image added. Gatekeeper and notarization were never affected.

### Install
Download **Vespertine-0.6.4.dmg**, or let Vespertine update itself. Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.
