**Vespertine 0.15.0 adds Transitions, an optional way to blend one song into the next (Crossfade, Smart Mix and Live Set), and lets the Visualizer draw in the colours of the album cover.**

### Transitions (off until you turn them on)
- **Off, it isn't there.** Transitions are a switch in Settings > Playback. While it is off, which it is by default, there is no button, menu item, playlist menu or setting for them beyond that one switch, and every song plays exactly as it did in 0.14.2, bit for bit.
- **Turn it on, and it's everywhere you play.** A Transitions button in the transport bar, Up Next, the mini player and the menu bar window (its menu picks the style and length), Controls > Transitions (⌥⌘X), and a Transitions choice in each playlist's menu. Up Next shows how each song will join the one before it.
- **Crossfade.** One song fades into the next over 1 to 12 seconds, with a curve you choose. Silence at the ends of songs can be trimmed, and you can put a gap of up to 10 seconds between songs instead. With transitions on, play, pause, stop, skip and seek fade in or out in a fraction of a second rather than starting or stopping dead.
- **Smart Mix.** Vespertine measures the ends of your songs and picks a blend for each pair: it follows a real fade-out, trims silence, cuts quickly between hard endings and starts, and matches loudness inside the blend. When it is unsure, it does a plain crossfade.
- **Live Set.** For hosting: Smart Mix at every song, skips that always blend, one output rate for the whole session (48 or 96 kHz), and beat-matched blends between songs with a steady tempo, with an optional bass swap.
- **Where it never blends.** Albums, CUE sheets, SACDs, classical and other excluded genres, songs read live from a network share, and playlists you turn it off for always play gapless. Between two native DSD songs at the same rate, a gap is filled with DSD silence so your DAC stays locked in DSD.
- **Bit-perfect first.** By default a transition never changes your output format; a song that would need another rate fades out and in instead. "Transitions first" (resample, DSD as PCM) is a setting you choose.

### Visualizer
- **Album Art colours.** A new colour theme draws every style in the colours of the cover of the song playing, fading to the next cover's over under a second. A cover with no colour, or no cover, uses the Vespertine colours. The peak and clip lights on the meters stay red.
- **Atmos wording.** Settings now says exactly which meters do not apply to Dolby Atmos rendered by macOS.

### Verification
- The engine, analysis and app test suites pass. With Transitions off, the 23 recorded checks of what the engine hands the output device are byte for byte the same as 0.14.2's, and the Crossfade, Smart Mix and Live Set results are checked against recorded values too.
- Not yet checked: nobody has listened to a beat-matched Live Set blend yet, and the bass swap, Live Set's fixed rate and the DSD gap have not been run through a real DAC. Beat detection finds a steady tempo at about four song ends in ten, so many Live Set blends line up beats but not bars. The Atmos dip is not done: Atmos boundaries stay cuts. The translations of the new Transitions text have not been reviewed by native speakers.

### Install
Download **Vespertine-0.15.0.dmg**, or let Vespertine update itself. Requires macOS 14.4 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled. Homebrew: `brew upgrade --cask vespertine`.
