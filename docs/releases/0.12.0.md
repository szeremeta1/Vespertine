**Vespertine now plays DST-compressed DSDIFF files and the LPCM music on DVD-Video discs natively, keeps every track of EAC CUE sheets, and starts songs from a network share right away even while a library scan is running.**

### DST-compressed DSDIFF
- **Plays natively.** Compressed `.dff` files used to go to a decoder that couldn't read DST, so they failed with “Decoder not found”. They now go through the same lossless DST decoder that SACD images use, so DST DSDIFF plays as DSD over DoP bit for bit, with BIT-PERFECT shown as for a DSF file, or through the usual DSD → PCM conversion.
- **Damage fails clearly.** Vespertine checks every frame's bounds, the optional DSDIFF index and its CRCs. A damaged file reports an error instead of playing silence.
- **Library upgrade.** On the first launch, DSDIFF files the library already holds are read again, so their durations and formats are right. Track IDs, play counts and playlists are kept.
- **Checked on real files.** All 59 DST DSDIFF files in a real collection, with stereo, 5- and 6-channel areas, decoded to the same DSD as an independent reference.

### DVD-Video LPCM
- **Discs play straight from the image.** Add a DVD-Video image (`.iso`) or a folder holding `VIDEO_TS`, and its LPCM music appears as albums: one per title, one track per chapter. Nothing is mounted, extracted or written.
- **Supported formats.** 16-, 20- and 24-bit audio at 48 and 96 kHz, 1 to 8 channels, within DVD-Video's limits.
- **Exact timing.** Timing comes from the disc's 90 kHz time stamps, not its rounded chapter times. Seeks land on the exact sample, and tracks that share a VOB join without a gap.
- **Damaged navigation.** On discs with inconsistent navigation, the tracks are rebuilt from the disc's own navigation packs. Menus and short preview clips are left out.
- **Not supported.** DVD-Audio, including MLP and the `AUDIO_TS` folder, is not supported. A DVD-Audio disc says so and adds nothing. Encrypted (CSS) discs need a decrypted copy.
- **Checked on real discs.** A 26-track DVD image and a two-album `VIDEO_TS` folder decoded sample for sample like independent extractions.
- **Details:** [DVD-Video LPCM](https://github.com/szeremeta1/Vespertine/blob/main/docs/DVD-VIDEO-LPCM.md).

### Network shares
- **Songs start right away during a scan.** While a library scan is reading a network share, a song from that share used to take up to 40 seconds to start or seek, and could stall. Now, while that song is starting, seeking or playing from the share, the scan steps back to one request at a time so the song's reads go first. The scan still finishes, and goes back to full speed once the song plays from its local copy or stops.

### Fixed
- **CUE sheets from EAC.** When a CUE sheet named its audio file after a track's `TRACK` or `INDEX 00` line, the track could be lost or given to the wrong file. Each track now belongs to the file that holds its `INDEX 01`, pregap included.
- **Sidebar at launch.** Opening a library no longer briefly shows the empty-library screen, or makes the sidebar fill in row by row. The playlists and sources are there from the first frame, which also removes an AppKit warning that would become a crash in a future macOS.

### Known issues
- DVD-Video tracks are named “Track N”, because the format has no song titles. Rename them in the tag editor.
- Only the first LPCM stream of a DVD title plays. 20- and 24-bit DVD audio with 3 or more channels hasn't been checked on a real disc yet.
- A stray icon can appear next to the sidebar button while the search field is active.

### Install
Download **Vespertine-0.12.0.dmg**, or let Vespertine update itself. Requires macOS 14.4 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled. Homebrew: `brew upgrade --cask vespertine`.
