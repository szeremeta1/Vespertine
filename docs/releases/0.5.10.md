**Changes on your server show up by themselves.**

### Improved
- **Music added to or deleted from a network share appears in Nocturne on its own.** Connected shares are rescanned every half hour (only new or changed files are read, and never while you're playing from the share), and right after the server's `nocturne-analyze` run. Before, a share was only rescanned after being offline for a day. **File → Rescan Library** still does it immediately.
- Deleted tracks now disappear from regular playlists too, not just from albums, songs, search and smart playlists. They stay in the playlist and come back if the files are restored.

### Install
Download **Nocturne-0.5.10.dmg**, or let Nocturne update itself. Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.
