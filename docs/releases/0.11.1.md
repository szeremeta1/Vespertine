**Album editions now share one card, and later tracks in DSD CUE albums play without the region-decoder crash.**

### Album editions
- **One card per album.** Recognized remasters, mono and stereo releases, SACDs and other tagged editions appear together in the library, artist, genre, source, recent and search views.
- **Choose the edition.** Open the album to select a release with Choose Edition or its edition card. Each release keeps its own tracks, artwork and format.
- **Page playback plays each album once.** Play, Shuffle and queue actions select one matching edition per album, including on filtered pages.
- **Music stays in place.** Grouping changes the presentation; it does not move, delete or retag your files.

### Fixed
- **DSD CUE playback.** CUE offsets are converted from the native DSD frame rate into the decoder's PCM or DoP frame rate before opening a track. Later tracks no longer use offsets beyond the decoder's end.
- **Invalid CUE regions.** An empty or out-of-range region returns a playback error instead of aborting the app. Final-track lengths remain clamped to the audio available.

### Install
Download **Vespertine-0.11.1.dmg**, or let Vespertine update itself. Requires macOS 14.4 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled. Homebrew: `brew upgrade --cask vespertine`.
