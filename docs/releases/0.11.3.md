**With exclusive access, Vespertine no longer changes the volume of the Mac's other sound output unless you ask it to, and the loopback test and 16-bit setting are now translated into every language.**

### Volume keys with exclusive access
- **Off by default.** While Vespertine holds a DAC exclusively, macOS moves the Mac's sound output to another device, often the built-in speakers. Vespertine used to copy the DAC's volume onto that output so the volume keys kept working, which meant other apps' sounds could play from the speakers at the DAC's level. Now it leaves that output's volume alone.
- **Your choice.** **Settings › Playback › Output › Volume keys control the device held exclusively** brings the old behaviour back: the volume keys, Control Center and the AirPods Max Digital Crown change the DAC's volume by way of the other output. Turning it off gives that output its own volume back at once. The setup assistant's "Volume keys follow Vespertine" switch sets it while exclusive access is on.
- **Clearer descriptions.** Settings, the output menu and the setup assistant now say what the volume keys do in each case.

### Translations
- **The loopback test is translated** in all 44 languages: the window, its menu item, the quick and full runs, the verdicts and Copy Report, and the loopback lines in the signal path.
- **So are Match 16-bit sources** and its explanation, and the new volume setting.

### Fixed
- **Long labels fit.** German "Abweichend" and the other languages' "Mixed" in the tag editor's disc and track fields, Brazilian Portuguese "Adicionadas Recentemente" in the sidebar, and long custom tag names such as MUSICBRAINZ_ALBUMARTISTID are no longer cut off.
- **Tag editor wording.** With only CUE or SACD disc-image tracks selected, the tag editor says the tags are saved in Vespertine's library instead of "Writes tags to 0 files".

### Known issues
- A stray icon can appear next to the sidebar button while the search field is active.
- Some long translations of "Recently Added" can still be cut off when the sidebar is at its narrowest.
