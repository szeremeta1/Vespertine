**Vespertine 1.2.1.** The free, open-source Mac player for surround and hi-res music collections.

If you're new here: Vespertine plays your stereo and surround music from one library (FLAC, DSD, SACD and DVD-Video images, DTS CDs, Dolby TrueHD, DTS-HD Master Audio, Atmos from Dolby Digital Plus), shows every step between the file and your DAC, measures and corrects your room with a USB microphone, and tells you whether your "hi-res" files really are. No account, no telemetry, no subscription.

### New

- **One song, not two.** If you have a song in stereo and in surround or Dolby Atmos, on one album or on two editions of it, it is now listed once in Songs, Favorites and search, and plays once in a shuffle. Vespertine plays the version that suits the output you are using. A heart on one version is a heart on the song. To hear a particular version, open the song's list of copies and play it there. On by default; the switch "Treat a song's stereo and surround versions as one song" is in Settings › Playback, under Surround formats; off, every file is listed and plays as itself.
- **AirPlay Players (beta): AirPlay 2 receivers.** Receivers that only take AirPlay 2 can now be chosen as an output. One that shows a code on its screen asks for it once; Forget Pairing removes what was saved. A receiver that says it takes 24-bit or 48 kHz audio is sent a 24-bit or 48 kHz song as it is, and CD-quality songs go exactly as before. Tested against Vespertine's own test receiver, not yet against real ones: reports wanted. Still off by default, in Settings › Playback.
- **AirPlay Players (beta): more receivers work.** Receivers with a password can be chosen: Vespertine asks for the password and keeps it in the macOS Keychain (Forget Password removes it). Third-party speakers and AV receivers that were listed as "not supported yet" can now be chosen. Receivers that show covers get the song's album art.
- **Speaker Groups (beta): a room in 3D.** The room in the speaker group editor can be turned, zoomed and seen from above, the back or the side. Speakers have a height, the room a ceiling, and listening is one seat or an area of the floor. There are layouts from stereo to 9.1.6, and a group's outputs can be added, removed and reordered in the editor. Still off by default.
- **Speaker Groups (beta): Fill the room.** Every speaker now plays the whole song, so no part of the room is left with half of it. Two styles: Stage in front, and Even everywhere. It works for surround songs and through Mac Sound Output too.

### Fixed

- The six switches in Settings › Library, Local Library among them, now say their names to VoiceOver.
- A song sent to a network player from a file that was still being read could be cut short as if it were over. Vespertine now reads again, and drops the connection if the file stays unreadable.

### iPhone and iPad

The iPhone and iPad app is a separate download and ships on its own schedule. The Mac side of what it does now: the phone can set the Mac's equalizer, room correction, Spatial Audio and balance; tapping a song in a playlist plays from that song; long playlists open a page at a time; and the cover in Now Playing shows for albums with very large artwork.

### Install

Download **Vespertine-1.2.1.dmg**, or `brew install --cask szeremeta1/tap/vespertine`. macOS 14.4 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.

Already installed? Vespertine updates itself, or run `brew upgrade --cask vespertine`.
