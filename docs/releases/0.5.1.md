**Receivers, surround speakers and network shares, done properly.**

### Improved
- **AV receivers and multichannel interfaces.**
  - A receiver or TV left in 2-channel mode over HDMI is still recognised as an 8-channel output, and Nocturne switches it to the channels it needs.
  - When HDMI carries 8 channels only up to 96 kHz, higher-rate surround plays every channel at 96 kHz. Nocturne never falls back to a stereo downmix to keep the higher rate.
  - Speaker setups from Audio MIDI Setup → Configure Speakers are followed by position. 5.1 plays in a 7.1 room with the back speakers silent, and 7.1 folds into a 5.1 room so every channel is heard. HDMI's own channel order is handled.
  - Devices that spread their channels over several hardware streams now work, including many interfaces and aggregate devices.
- **Rich multichannel info.**
  - Now Playing shows a live meter for every channel (L, R, C, LFE, Ls, Rs…).
  - It says where each channel goes: the speakers, the downmix or the virtual speakers of Spatial Audio.
  - The output panel shows each device's configured speakers.
  - If a multichannel output has no speaker setup configured, it points you to the one setting that fixes that.
- **Network shares ride out stalls.** When a slow read from a share can't keep up, playback holds in silence and picks up exactly where it stopped once two seconds are buffered, instead of stuttering. "Buffering from the network…" shows while it waits.

### Install
Download **Nocturne-0.5.1.dmg**, or let Nocturne update itself. Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.
