**Library analysis keeps more network reads in flight.**

### Improved
- **Analysis reads up to six files from a network share at once**, up from two, so waiting on one slow read no longer holds up the rest. How much faster that makes a full-library analysis depends on your network: it helps most on shares that respond slowly, and it can't exceed what your connection to the share can carry. Local files stay at two at a time, which is plenty for the processor.
- **Playback comes first.** While you're playing music from a share, analysis drops back to two network files at a time, so what you're listening to always has the bandwidth it needs.

### Install
Download **Nocturne-0.5.4.dmg**, or let Nocturne update itself. Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.
