**Vespertine works with VoiceOver and the keyboard, Favorites keep a song's stereo and 5.1 versions together, and every fix from a full code audit.**

### New
- **Vespertine works with VoiceOver and the keyboard.**
  - Every icon-only button, menu and picker has a name, and shuffle and repeat say whether they're on.
  - The seek bar and volume are adjustable with VoiceOver and Full Keyboard Access.
  - Album cards take keyboard focus; Return or Space opens them.
  - Each queue row reads as one item, and Play Now is in its context menu and VoiceOver actions.
  - Errors are announced, and stay up for VoiceOver users until dismissed.
  - The device chip and mini player say Bit-perfect or Converted in words, not only in colour.
- **Reduce Motion is respected.** The playing bars stand still, the spectrum slows, and the inspector and error banner fade in place.
- **New Controls menu commands:** Skip Forward and Back 10 Seconds (⌥⌘→ and ⌥⌘←) and Go to Current Album (⌘J).

### Changed
- **Favorites hold songs, not files.** Hearting a song covers every version of it on its album, so its stereo and 5.1 versions are favorites together, including versions scanned later and favorites you already have. The Favorites page lists each song once, in the version that suits the current output, with "+ 5.1" or "+ STEREO" beside it, and Favorites filtered to multichannel shows the 5.1 versions.
- **Secondary text is easier to read.** Its contrast is now at least 4.5:1 on every background.
- **Updates are checked before they're opened.** Sparkle verifies an update's signature before it extracts the download.
- **Connect to a Server warns about WebDAV over http**, which sends the name, password and music unencrypted.

### Fixed
- **Removing a network share can no longer delete music on the server.** If the share couldn't be unmounted, removing it could walk into the still-mounted share and delete its contents. Vespertine now only ever removes an empty mount folder.
- **One unreadable folder no longer stops a scan.** A folder inside a source that can't be listed is reported and skipped, and nothing under it is marked missing.
- **Large libraries and slow shares stay responsive.** Draining a large analysis queue no longer stalls the app, and a share that hangs while reading can't hold up everything else.
- **Dolby audio in MP4 and M4A files is read safely.** Frames that arrive in several pieces are no longer read past the end of the first one.
- **Opening a song from Finder is faster**: it no longer loads the whole library to find the track.

### Under the hood
- The app is built against a committed lockfile, so every release uses exactly the same versions of its libraries.
- The full license texts of every bundled component now ship inside the app, and the FFmpeg source is attached to each release.
- Release tooling is pinned by hash, and CI actions by commit.
- `release.sh --install` asks a running Vespertine to quit, so the DAC's format and the volume keys are put back, instead of killing it.

### Install
Download **Vespertine-0.8.0.dmg**, or let Vespertine update itself. Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled. Homebrew: `brew upgrade --cask vespertine`.
