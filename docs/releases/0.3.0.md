**Find your music, and make its metadata rich.**

### New
- **Find Music on This Mac…** (File menu, ⇧⌘F, or the empty-library screen) searches every indexed drive with Spotlight and checks each file's *real* format with Nocturne's decoder.
  - Results are grouped by folder and filterable by **Hi-Res**, **Lossless** or **All Music**.
  - Voice recordings, telephony audio, prompts and short clips are left out automatically.
  - Identical copies in different folders are recognized and left unticked.
- **Import & Organize with APFS clones.** Copies go into `~/Music/Nocturne` as Artist / Album / Track and take no extra disk space on the same drive. Your originals are never changed. Untagged files are named and tagged from structured file names such as `Artist - Album - 01 Title.wav`.
- **Enrich Metadata…** (⇧⌘E, or an album's ⋯ menu) fills in missing titles, artists, albums, years, track numbers, genres and cover art. It works from file names and [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org), with covers from the Cover Art Archive (falling back to other editions of the same album).
  - It uses MusicBrainz IDs that are already in your files.
  - Every proposal shows its source and confidence. Only near-certain matches are pre-selected, and existing tags are never overwritten unless you ask.
- **Skip voice recordings and short clips** (Settings → Library) keeps non-music out of scanned folders.

### Fixed
- **A crash when scanning folders with many MP3 files.** The MP3 decoder isn't safe to open from several threads at once.
- **Release dates on WAV, AIFF and MP3 files.** They could be silently dropped or shifted by a year, and later edits could erase them.
- **Temporary MusicBrainz outages** are retried instead of being reported as "no match".

### Install
Download **Nocturne-0.3.0.dmg**, or let Nocturne 0.2.x update itself. Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.
