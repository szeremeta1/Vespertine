**Favorites, Spatial Audio for every speaker layout, and no more freezes when you press play.**

### New
- **Favorites.** Click the heart beside a song to keep it in **Favorites**, a new page under *Library* in the sidebar, newest first, with *Play* and *Shuffle*.
  - The heart is there while you listen: next to the title in the playback bar, in Now Playing, in the mini player and in the menu bar player. Controls → *Add Current Song to Favorites* (⌘L) does the same.
  - Song lists have a quiet heart column: favorites show a filled heart, and on other songs an outline appears only on the row you point at, select or play. Every song's menu has *Add to Favorites* too, and you can drop an album on *Favorites* in the sidebar.
  - A heart clicked by mistake comes back with ⌘Z.
  - Favorites belong to your library, like play counts and playlists; nothing is written into your files. A favorite stays with its song when the file is moved or renamed.
  - Smart playlists can use them: *Is Favorite* is a new rule ("favorites in 24-bit", for example).
- **Dolby Digital in Matroska audio files (`.mka`) plays.**

### Improved
- **Spatial Audio places every channel on its own speaker, whatever the layout.** Each channel goes to its own speaker in the smallest layout that holds them all, and Now Playing names the layout from its speakers ("3.1", "4.0", "Quad"). Layouts other than the standard ones used to be folded on the way to Apple's spatial renderer:
  - 2.1, 3.1 and 4.1 lost their LFE;
  - LCRS smeared its centre channels, and wide and height speakers fell into the front pair;
  - 7.1 files with WAVE channel masks swapped their side and rear pairs;
  - layouts with more than 8 channels had no positions at all.
- **Short album tracks stay in your library.** Vespertine leaves out files under 45 seconds, so voice prompts and sound effects don't fill your library. That also dropped real album tracks: interludes, intros, hidden-track snippets. A short track tagged as part of an album (album name and track number) is now kept; untagged clips, one-track jingles and voice recordings still aren't. Tracks left out before appear on the next rescan.

### Fixed
- **Pressing play could freeze the window for seconds.** As a song started, macOS reported a change of output device, and Vespertine re-read every device's formats on the main thread while the DAC was still switching. Device reads and volume changes now happen in the background.
- **A slow or stalled network share could freeze the window,** while Vespertine checked the share or reconnected it. That work now runs in the background with a time limit, as does reading the server's analysis results.
- **Some Dolby Digital and Dolby Digital Plus files played with channels in the wrong places** (layouts 2/1, 3/0 + LFE and 3/1 + LFE): macOS's decoder moves channels around, drops the LFE or repeats a channel in these three modes. Those files are now decoded with FFmpeg, at full dynamic range; everything else stays with macOS, and the signal path says which decoder is used and why.
- **24-bit TTA files could crash Vespertine.** The TTA decoder wrote one byte past the end of its buffer.

### Install
Download **Vespertine-0.6.1.dmg**, or let Vespertine update itself. Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.
