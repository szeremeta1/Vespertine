**Moved and renamed music keeps playing, AirPods Max come back to Nocturne on their own, and shuffle and repeat no longer interrupt the music.**

### Fixed
- **Music that was moved or renamed on a share or drive plays again.** When a library manager such as Lidarr reorganizes files (for example `Artist - Album [FLAC]/02. Song.flac` becoming `Artist/Album (1973)/CD 01/Artist - Album - 02 - Song.flac`), Nocturne now recognizes each moved file as the same song. Its playlists, play count, rating, date added and analysis move with it, and the old copy's entry, which could no longer play ("No such file or directory"), goes away. Songs in the play queue are pointed at their new location too.
- If a song can't be played because its file is gone, Nocturne rescans that source right away (even while music is playing) instead of waiting for the next scheduled rescan, and tells you what's happening instead of showing a raw file error.
- **Taking AirPods Max off and putting them back on no longer moves your music to the FiiO or the Mac's speakers.** macOS removes the AirPods as an audio device while they're off your head and adds them back a few seconds after you put them on. The AirPods' "play" command often arrives before that, and Nocturne used to fall back to whatever other output was available, then point the Mac's sound output there too. Now Nocturne never swaps the output you chose for another one. If it isn't there yet, Nocturne waits for it for up to a minute and starts playing the moment it's back, so you no longer need to reselect the AirPods in Control Center or in Nocturne. Pressing pause cancels the wait.
- If the output you chose drops out in the middle of a song (a DAC re-enumerating, AirPods reconnecting), playback continues where it left off once it returns, instead of stopping with "disconnected".
- Pressing **Shuffle**, **Repeat** or **Repeat One** (or adding, removing or reordering songs in the queue) no longer causes a brief dropout. The song that's playing carries on untouched; only the song lined up after it changes.
- Turning on **Repeat** while the last song in the queue is playing now continues into the repeat as expected.

### Install
Download **Nocturne-0.5.13.dmg**, or let Nocturne update itself. Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.
