**DSD at every rate, format badges, and a crash fix for damaged files.**

### New
- **Format badges.** Now Playing names the format of what's playing: Dolby Atmos, Dolby TrueHD, Dolby Digital Plus, Dolby Digital, DTS-HD Master Audio, DTS, DSD64–DSD512, Hi-Res Lossless or Lossless. Album pages show them too, and the Albums filter has a **Dolby & DTS** option. They're plain text in Nocturne's own style, not the companies' logos (see *Trademarks* in the README).
- **Files that can't hold tags** (bare `.ac3`, `.ec3`, `.dts`, `.thd` streams) take their title from the file name and their album from the folder, instead of all landing in one "Unknown Album".
- **Album pages say broadly where an album lives** ("High-Res Music › Pink Floyd") instead of its whole folder path.
- **Albums in several formats** say "Mixed · up to 24/96" instead of naming whichever format sorted last.

- **Stereo and surround versions of the same album play the right one automatically.** When an album has each song in stereo and in 5.1 (an SACD's two layers, for example), it lists each song once and plays the surround version on a multichannel output or with Spatial Audio on (AirPods), and the stereo one on a stereo DAC or with Spatial Audio off. Switch outputs and the upcoming songs follow. *Settings → Playback → Stereo and surround versions* can pin it to either.
- **The Now Playing cover makes room.** It shrinks to fit so the title, badges and signal path show without scrolling, and grows back on a taller window.

### Fixed
- **DSD128 and above played nothing, and DSDIFF (`.dff`) above DSD64 wouldn't open.** All DSD now goes through FFmpeg's DSD reader: DSD64 to DSD512 in DSF and DSDIFF, sent as DoP to DACs that take it at that rate and converted to PCM otherwise.
- **A damaged Monkey's Audio (APE) file could quit Nocturne** when you skipped within it. Errors thrown inside codec libraries are now caught and reported as a file that won't play.
- **The spectrum and channel meters work while DSD plays as DoP.** They used to go flat, because DoP frames carry DSD bits rather than PCM. They now read the DSD inside each frame (display only; the frames still reach the DAC untouched).
- **SACD rips find their cover** when the tracks sit in "Multichannel 5.1" and "Stereo" folders and the cover one level up, as they already did for "CD 1" folders.
- **Albums spread over several folders play in track order** (they're only listed folder by folder when track numbers repeat, as with an SACD's two layers).
- **A cover added to an album's folder later now shows up** on the next rescan. Tracks without art used to be skipped because the audio files themselves hadn't changed.
- **Skipping, choosing another song, seeking, pausing or switching outputs silences the old audio immediately.** The old song used to play on from Nocturne's 20–30 s buffer until the new one was ready, which on a busy share could be several seconds.
- **Songs on a share start sooner.** DTS, TrueHD, Matroska and DSD files were analysed for up to 5 MB before playing, on every start and every output switch; the headers say all that's needed.
- **Switching outputs mid-song takes a fraction of a second, even on a busy share.** The song's file stays open and simply continues from the same spot on the new output (it used to be opened and analysed again, which took up to half a minute on a loaded server). A new command no longer waits behind the first second of buffering, retrying an output that isn't ready yet (AirPods Max just put back on) doesn't reopen the file, several quick switches restart playback once, and every switch, start and device failure is logged.
- **The progress bar starts from zero on the new song.** Skipping used to show the previous song's position (and sometimes its title) until the new one had loaded; a seek no longer jumps back before it lands.
- **Nocturne no longer updates macOS's Now Playing info several times a second** while playing; macOS extrapolates the time itself.
- **MusicBrainz Lookup and tag edits work on read-only shares.** Nocturne used to copy each file for a backup before discovering it couldn't write it, which on a share full of DSD looked like a hang. Files it can't write now get the changes in its library instead (kept through rescans, revertible), and the editor says so. The lookup shows its progress.
- **DSF albums show their year.** The tag reader dropped the release and original dates from DSF files' ID3 tags; Nocturne now reads them itself.
- **SACD rips with separate "Multichannel" and "Stereo" folders list one layer after the other** instead of interleaving tracks with the same numbers.
- **Now Playing fits its panel.** Long status badges ("SPATIAL · HEAD TRACKED") wrap instead of spilling over the track list, and song lists no longer need a sideways scroll with the inspector open.
- **DTS CDs no longer show "1411k".** That's the bitrate of the CD audio carrying the DTS stream, not of the DTS stream itself.
- **Atmos in TrueHD and DTS:X say what's decoded.** Nocturne plays their lossless channel bed; the signal path now says the objects aren't rendered instead of implying they are.

### Install
Download **Nocturne-0.5.19.dmg**, or let Nocturne update itself. Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.
