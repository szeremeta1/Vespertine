**Vespertine 1.2.2.** The free, open-source Mac player for surround and hi-res music collections.

If you're new here: Vespertine plays your stereo and surround music from one library (FLAC, DSD, SACD and DVD-Video images, DTS CDs, Dolby TrueHD, DTS-HD Master Audio, Atmos from Dolby Digital Plus), shows every step between the file and your DAC, measures and corrects your room with a USB microphone, and tells you whether your "hi-res" files really are. No account, no telemetry, no subscription.

### New

- **Choose your columns.** In Songs and Favorites, Control-click the column headers to choose which columns show, as in Music. New columns: Last Played, Date Added, BPM, Skips and Last Skipped. Every column sorts, and the columns you chose, their widths and their order are kept for the next launch.
- **Skips.** Pressing Next on a song that has played for at least 2 seconds, but not long enough to count as a play, now counts as a skip, the way Music counts them. Skips and Last Skipped are columns in song lists, hidden until you choose them.
- **Your listening statistics are kept.** Play counts, last played, ratings, date added, favorites, skips and play history now survive a rescan, a file moved to another folder, and a folder taken out of the library and added again. Remove Missing Songs is the one command that forgets them, and its confirmation says so.
- **AirPlay Players (beta): 24-bit / 48 kHz to an Apple TV 4K.** Songs above CD quality are now sent to an Apple TV 4K as 24-bit / 48 kHz, and Sent as in the signal path says so. This has been heard on a real Apple TV 4K. If a receiver doesn't take that stream, or never starts playing it, the song picks up again from where that stream began, as 16-bit / 44.1 kHz. Still off by default, in Settings › Playback.

### Fixed

- AirPlay Players (beta): an Apple TV 4K on tvOS 27 refused every song after pairing. It plays now.
- Previous on a song from a network share, skipped while it was still loading, failed with "The operation was cancelled". It plays now.
- On a DAC known not to play DSD over DoP at some rates (the FiiO K11 at the 48 kHz-family DSD rates), those songs are now converted to PCM instead of being sent as DoP it can't play.
- Speaker Groups (beta): on a group with one pair of surround speakers, a 7.1 song's side surrounds played partly from the front speakers. They now stay on the surround speakers. A surround song played on fewer speakers than it has channels can no longer clip them; nothing is turned down unless it would.
- A song list in a window that had been closed could change which song was selected.
- Remote: a paired phone now has to prove which phone it is when it connects, so one paired phone can't sign in as another, and a removed phone can't stay on under another's name. Pairing by typed code is protected against someone who recorded it on your network (when the phone app is up to date too).
- Web remote: it now takes at most 48 connections at once, and closes one that doesn't finish its request within 10 seconds.

### iPhone and iPad

The iPhone and iPad app is a separate download and ships on its own schedule. The Mac side of what it does now: the phone can sort Songs and Favorites by title, artist, album, plays or date, and the Mac checks each phone's identity when it signs in.

### Install

Download **Vespertine-1.2.2.dmg**, or `brew install --cask szeremeta1/tap/vespertine`. macOS 14.4 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.

Already installed? Vespertine updates itself, or run `brew upgrade --cask vespertine`.
