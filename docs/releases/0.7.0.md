**DSD without pops, honest fake hi-res verdicts that also catch ffmpeg upsamples, and dozens of fixes from a pre-launch audit.**

### New
- **DSD over DoP keeps the DAC locked in DSD.** Pause, seek, skip, a network stall or an underrun now send DSD silence instead of zeros, so the DAC no longer drops out of DSD and re-locks with a pop. A paused DSD track keeps the DAC in DSD until "Release device after pausing for" runs out.
- **Notification sounds stay at their usual loudness during exclusive playback.** While Vespertine holds a DAC, the volume keys still step the DAC from its real level, and alerts and notification sounds play from the Mac's speakers as loud as they were before, not at the DAC's level. Everything is put back when playback stops, and on the next launch after a crash. Apps that play their sounds as ordinary audio (Slack, Discord, browser tabs) still play at the speakers' level.
- **Upsampled files are caught by their mirror image.** A 44.1 or 48 kHz master upsampled with an imperfect filter (ffmpeg's default, among others) leaves a reflection of the music above 22 or 24 kHz. Vespertine now looks for it, so `ffmpeg -ar 96000` fakes are flagged.

### Changed
- **Fake hi-res verdicts are questions, not accusations.** Spectral findings read "LOSSY ORIGIN?", "UPSAMPLED?" or "SYNTHETIC HIGH FREQUENCIES?" at POSSIBLE or LIKELY, and each names its innocent explanations (steep mastering filters, FM sources, historical masters). Only zero padding is reported as certain.
  - Tape hiss and vinyl noise above a cutoff are no longer called synthetic.
  - A plain MP3's top band reads as a lossy origin.
  - Short or silent files are inconclusive.
  - Your library is re-judged on first launch, and hi-res files judged genuine are analyzed once more in the background. NAS users: update `vespertine-analyze` so the server does that instead.
- **Network shares are left alone while Vespertine isn't in use**, so a NAS's disks can sleep.
  - Coming back to Vespertine rescans a share that hasn't been checked for half an hour.
  - A share you eject stays ejected until you choose Reconnect.
- **The Mac's speakers, and virtual or aggregate devices, are never labelled BIT-PERFECT.**

### Fixed
- **Playback**
  - Mono files play on both speakers.
  - A sample rate changed by another app no longer plays the song at the wrong speed.
  - Gapless DSD no longer clicks between tracks.
  - A CD rip is never mistaken for a DTS CD.
  - The last song plays out to the end on Bluetooth and AirPlay.
  - A damaged ReplayGain tag can't blast.
  - Digital volume follows the output that's playing, and works when turned on mid-song in integer mode.
  - 32-bit float files are never sent as integers.
- **Library**
  - Albums by artists with accented capitals (Édith Piaf, MØ) play.
  - Compilations without an Album Artist are one album.
  - Importing the same folder twice copies nothing new.
  - A short track imported on purpose stays in the library.
  - Changes made during a scan aren't dropped.
  - Adding the folder of a song opened with Open With takes the song in.
  - CUE sheets in Japanese and Cyrillic read correctly.
- **Tags**
  - Tag edits keep every value of fields that have several.
  - A crash can't leave half-written files, and the playing song isn't disturbed.
  - Tag backups stay under 5 GB.
- **Large libraries** stay responsive: no full re-sort per click, and selections follow the songs, not the rows.
- **Network shares**
  - A wrong password isn't retried every 45 seconds.
  - A share added read-only is never written.
  - A share whose connection died is mounted afresh.
- **Smaller fixes**
  - An album page follows an edit to its title.
  - Settings opens its panels in front.
  - Removing a source or deleting a playlist asks first.

### Install
Download **Vespertine-0.7.0.dmg**, or let Vespertine update itself. Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.
