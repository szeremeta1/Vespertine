**Multichannel music, and Spatial Audio on AirPods.**

### New
- **Every multichannel file plays properly.** 5.0, 5.1, 7.1 and other layouts (the kind found on SACD, DVD-Audio and Blu-ray rips and Atmos-derived releases) now play from start to finish, each channel where it belongs:
  - **AirPods and Beats: Spatial Audio.** Nocturne renders the channels as virtual speakers around you with Apple's own spatial renderer, using your Personalized Spatial Audio profile if you've set one up. **Head Tracked** keeps the speakers in place as you turn your head; **Fixed** keeps them in front of you. Rendering happens as the audio reaches your headphones, so head tracking responds instantly.
  - **Multichannel interfaces and receivers:** every channel goes to its own speaker, following the speaker layout in Audio MIDI Setup.
  - **Stereo DACs:** a standard downmix that respects each file's channel layout.
  - Choose per device in the output menu: **Spatial Audio: Head Tracked / Fixed / Off**. It's on (head tracked) for AirPods and Beats, off elsewhere.
  - The signal path shows exactly what happens ("SPATIAL · HEAD TRACKED", "5.1 → STEREO", or discrete channels), and never claims bit-perfect for a mix.
- **Export for Spatial Audio…** (an album's ⋯ menu): turn multichannel music into
  - **Spatial stereo (binaural)**: 24-bit ALAC that sounds spatial on any AirPods or headphones, on any device and in any player; or
  - **Multichannel lossless**: 24-bit ALAC with every channel and its speaker layout, for Apple devices and players.
  Tags and cover art are copied, CUE-sheet tracks are handled, and your originals aren't changed.
- **Channel layouts everywhere**: formats read "FLAC · 24/88.2 · 5.1", albums show their layout, there's a **Multichannel** filter, and smart playlists can use **Channels**.

### Improved
- Tracks streamed from network shares buffer a little more before starting, so a slow first read never becomes a gap.
- Development builds are a separate app ("Nocturne Dev") with their own settings and library.

### Install
Download **Nocturne-0.5.0.dmg**, or let Nocturne update itself. Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.
