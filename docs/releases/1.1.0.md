**Vespertine 1.1.0.** The free, open-source Mac player for surround and hi-res music collections.

If you're new here: Vespertine plays your stereo and surround music from one library (FLAC, DSD, SACD and DVD-Video images, DTS CDs, Dolby TrueHD, DTS-HD Master Audio, Atmos from Dolby Digital Plus), shows every step between the file and your DAC, measures and corrects your room with a USB microphone, and tells you whether your "hi-res" files really are. No account, no telemetry, no subscription.

### New in 1.1.0

- **Phone remote.** Play, queue and browse your music from a phone, a tablet or a browser on your own network: playback, seeking, shuffle and repeat, the queue, albums, songs and playlists, favorites, search, outputs and volume. Pair a phone with a QR code or a 6-digit code; each paired phone has its own key, and removing it cuts it off. The web remote has its own switch and works in any browser on the network, over plain HTTP (Settings says so). For a Mac without a screen there are three more switches: open at login, keep the Mac awake while the remote is on, and wake for network access. Everything is off by default (Settings › Remote). The iPhone and iPad app, Vespertine Remote, is not on the App Store yet.
- **Mac Sound Output (beta): a speaker group as an output of the Mac.** Turn a speaker group into an output named as you named the group, so a browser, the TV app, Plex, IINA or VLC can play stereo, 5.1 or 7.1 to it, with the volume keys working, whether Vespertine is open or closed. Choose what it offers other apps (Automatic, Stereo, Quad, 5.1, 7.1, 7.1.4, 9.1.6) and what happens to stereo from other apps (as the group plays music, extended stereo, or upmixed to surround). The group's delays, levels, polarity and room correction apply as they do for music. It needs Vespertine's audio driver, installed from Settings with an administrator's password, and the microphone permission, because macOS counts the driver's input as recording. Off by default (Settings › Playback › Mac Sound Output (beta)). [Details and limits](https://github.com/szeremeta1/Vespertine/blob/main/docs/MAC-SOUND-OUTPUT.md)
- **DoP auto-setup.** DACs known to decode DSD over PCM have DoP on from the first launch, each with a published source. For another DAC that may take DoP, a "Play DSD natively?" card under the signal path plays a quiet 5-second DSD test clip, with the DAC's own volume capped at 20%, and asks whether you heard music or hiss. The DoP switch now also shows where DSD is converted to PCM. The list of DoP DACs updates itself about once a day from this project's releases (signed; Settings › Updates turns it off). [Details](https://github.com/szeremeta1/Vespertine/blob/main/docs/DOP-AUTO-SETUP.md)
- **The setup assistant** covers the phone remote, the DoP test and the DoP list, and, for anyone with a speaker group, Mac Sound Output.

### Fixed

- Import & Organize: "50 Cent - In da Club" is by 50 Cent, not track 50 by Cent; long titles in Hindi, Tamil, Thai, Japanese or Chinese no longer stop it; and it names the folder set in Settings.
- Chinese CUE sheets in GBK/GB 18030 read as Chinese, and a copied sheet keeps its encoding.
- A damaged Dolby frame header no longer ends the song.
- Shuffle: turning it off during a Play Next song keeps the album going. Play after the queue has ended starts from its first song.
- Search runs once, on the last keystroke, instead of on every key.
- ListenBrainz dates a listen from when the song started.
- A file with a very long name can have its tags edited; a playlist can't be renamed to an empty name; an M3U entry under a folder with a colon in its name is imported.
- Network shares: an address with an impossible port no longer crashes the app, and Connect says why an address can't be read.
- A silent track keeps its analysis result.
- Escape closes the Connect to a Server, Spatial Audio export, Transitions and Loopback Test sheets, and no longer closes the Spatial Audio export sheet in the middle of an export.
- Fill the room applies to the song that is playing.

### Known issues

- Mac Sound Output (beta): when stereo from other apps is upmixed to surround, the back channels play very quietly.
- Mac Sound Output (beta): with the output fixed at 5.1, the rear-surround channels of a 7.1 file are dropped instead of folded in.
- While Mac Sound Output is playing, the Now Playing panel shows "Speakers: Not set up".
- If the Mac Sound Output helper is quit by force, it has not yet been checked that it restarts by itself.

### Not tested yet

- Mac Sound Output on real DACs from the app: films and music from Safari, the TV app, IINA and VLC, 5.1 on a quad, the volume keys, the hand-off to Vespertine, and unplugging a DAC mid-film.
- The phone remote from a real iPhone, and the web remote in Safari and Chrome.
- The DoP test on a DAC that isn't on the list.
- Multichannel USB DACs and AV receivers (one Sony TV over HDMI has been tried). Dolby and DTS bitstream to a receiver is experimental.
- The new translations have not been reviewed by native speakers. Corrections are welcome.

If you have any of that hardware, a [device report](https://github.com/szeremeta1/Vespertine/issues/new?template=dac_report.yml) helps more than anything else.

### Install

Download **Vespertine-1.1.0.dmg**, or `brew install --cask szeremeta1/tap/vespertine`. macOS 14.4 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.

Already installed? Vespertine updates itself, or run `brew upgrade --cask vespertine`.
