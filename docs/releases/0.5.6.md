**Your server can do the analysis, playback always comes first, and browsing works like it should.**

### New
- **Genres,** like Apple Music: a Genres page in the sidebar (a tile per genre with its covers), and **Genre** and **Decade** menus on Albums (combined with the format chips) and a Genre menu on Songs. Messy tags are handled: "rock" and "Rock" and "Hip-Hop" and "Hip Hop" are the same genre, multi-genre tags ("Soul, Funk, R&B") count under each, old ID3 genre numbers become names, and Apple's localized genre names are merged into one.
- **Search by genre and year** as well as title and artist ("jazz", "1977", "miles 1959"); matching genres appear as their own row.
- **When Nocturne quits, it hands your DAC back** (Settings → Output): put it back to the sample rate and bit depth it had before Nocturne changed it (the default, so other apps and tools like LosslessSwitcher carry on where they left off), set it to 44.1 kHz · 16-bit or 48 kHz · 24-bit, or leave it as it is.
- **Server-side analysis.** For music on a NAS or server, run `nocturne-analyze` next to the files (see [How it works](https://github.com/szeremeta1/Vespertine/blob/main/docs/ANALYSIS.md#analyzing-on-the-server-vespertine-analyze)). Nocturne imports its results every few minutes, reading only what's new, and no longer reads that share's files over the network for background analysis. It runs the same analysis code as the app, with the same verdicts. Settings → Library shows the server's progress.
- **Search finds albums and artists too.** Results show matching artists and albums as rows of cards above the matching songs. Every word you type has to match, ignoring case and accents.

### Fixed
- **Album covers are always square.** Covers that aren't square (wide scans, box sets) no longer grow bigger than their neighbours or overlap them.
- **Going back keeps your place.** Open an album or artist, go back, and Albums, Artists, Songs, playlists and search results are exactly where you left them: same scroll position, sort and filters. Artist pages also no longer jump to the top while the library updates (for example, while analysis results come in).
- **Album cards work with VoiceOver:** open an album, or play it, from the grid.
- **Music on network shares plays smoothly while analysis runs.** Background analysis now never reads from a share while you're playing, or starting to play, a track from it. Any analysis in progress steps aside and resumes afterwards. Before, analysis could take most of a slow connection, so songs took a long time to start and then stuttered.
- **Songs from a share start faster.** Caching the current and next tracks now waits until a song has been playing for a few seconds, and downloads one file at a time while music streams. The song you're hearing keeps most of the connection.
- Analysis reads at most three files from a share at a time.

### Install
Download **Nocturne-0.5.6.dmg**, or let Nocturne update itself. Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.
