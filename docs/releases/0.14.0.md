**Vespertine 0.14.0 adds room correction: measure your speakers with a UMIK-1 microphone and play through filters made for your room. It also keeps network playback from running dry.**

### Room correction
- **Measure your room.** Put a calibrated UMIK-1 microphone at the listening seat and Vespertine plays test sweeps through each speaker in turn, at 1 to 5 spots (5 by default: the seat, then left, right, in front and behind). It checks the level and the route to every speaker first, rejects a bad run and lets you retry or pick up where you left off. Stereo and 5.1 are both supported.
- **Filters for each speaker.** From the measurement Vespertine builds a small set of filters per speaker, mostly cuts for the bass peaks a room adds. Choose a target (Warm is the default, or Gentle tilt or Flat) and a range (bass only, or full range) and the filters are refitted from the stored measurement without measuring again. The graph shows what was measured, what is predicted after correction and the target, and the prediction is labeled as a prediction.
- **Delay and level alignment.** Each speaker's delay and level are measured too, so nearer speakers are held back until the farthest one's sound arrives and louder ones are turned down to the quietest.
- **Bring your own filters.** Import an Equalizer APO or REW filter file (per-speaker delay, level and filters), or import impulse responses (WAV, AIFF, CAF or FLAC) to play through as convolution filters. Export a profile as Equalizer APO text or as a Vespertine profile. Lines Vespertine can't play are refused with the line number rather than played as something else.
- **Off by default, so bit-perfect stays bit-perfect.** Room correction is switched on per output and is tied to the output it was measured on. While it changes the samples, the signal path says ROOM CORRECTION and the stream is no longer marked bit-perfect. With it off, playback is untouched. Integer mode and Match 16-bit stay off while it is on.
- **What it covers.** Stereo and 5.1 PCM that Vespertine renders. Dolby and DTS bitstreams, Atmos and other channel counts step aside, and the signal path says why. DSD on a DoP DAC is converted to PCM while correction is on so it is corrected too; tick Keep native DSD on that output to send it natively and uncorrected instead.
- **One window.** View → Room Correction, also from the Equalizer window and each output's settings, holds the switch, Measure, Import, Export, the profiles, the before and after graph, the target and range, and each speaker's delay, level and filters. This replaces the earlier beta Room Measurement window. The method is written up in `docs/ROOM-CORRECTION.md`.

### Network playback
- **Fewer dropouts from a NAS.** Tracks on a network share are now copied to your Mac before they play, and the next track is copied ahead, so a hiccup on the network no longer stops the music. Large DSD files are read in a way that keeps a long lead ready.
- **A loading ring on the play button.** While Vespertine waits for data from the network, the play/pause button shows a small turning ring (still when Reduce Motion is on). Short read failures are retried.
- **Limits.** A very long outage, or a link that is slower than the music, can still make playback pause to refill. Cached copies use at most 8 GB, and a source larger than that fails with a clear message. See `docs/NETWORK-PLAYBACK.md`.

### Also new
- **DoP on by default for known DACs.** The FiiO K11 and the Fosi Audio ZD3 start with DoP turned on. If you turn it off, Vespertine remembers.
- **Recently Added shows 150 albums**, up from 60, so a big import doesn't push the albums just before it off the list.
- **Fosi Audio ZD3.** The ZD3 is now in the supported-devices list and the website screenshots were retaken on it.
- **Website.** A "Star it on GitHub" and "Join the discussion" line sits under the download buttons.

### Verification
- The engine, library and app test suites pass, including new tests for the room filter design, for playback through the correction stage and the convolution filter, for the measurement steps against a simulated room, for the import formats and for the network cache. The real-time audio code is also checked under the address, thread and undefined-behavior sanitizers.
- Not yet checked: playing through room correction on real hardware, and the measurement wizard and delay alignment with a real UMIK-1 and real speakers.

### Install
Download **Vespertine-0.14.0.dmg**, or let Vespertine update itself. Requires macOS 14.4 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled. Homebrew: `brew upgrade --cask vespertine`.
